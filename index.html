06 · Background

A physicist who ended up in operations.

I studied Physics and Electronics at the Federal University of Technology, Akure. That training taught me to look at how a system behaves, where it breaks, and why. I still work that way.

I started out keeping networks and servers running at Computer Warehouse Group, moved into telematics where hardware, data and customers meet, and from there into mobility and finance. For the past five years my work has centred on helping drivers own the vehicles they earn from and making sure the money behind that works for everyone involved.

Outside work, I'm drawn to the same questions: how cities move, how data can be shown so people actually use it, and what infrastructure emerging markets need next.

07 · How I work

Four habits behind the numbers.

  1. Start with the data

    Before I change anything I build the dashboard that shows what's actually happening. Most of my wins started as a chart someone hadn't seen yet.

  2. Process before scale

    Playbooks, SOPs and clean reconciliation come first. They're how one market becomes five without the quality dropping.

  3. Partners are relationships

    Banks, platforms and vendors stay when they trust you. I'm open with them, I bring the numbers, and I do what I said I'd do.

  4. Teams over heroes

    The results here came from Product, Risk, Compliance, CX and field teams pulling in one direction. My job is to make that easy.
